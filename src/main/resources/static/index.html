F1This list

        
Alt + 1 2 3Customer 1, 2 or 3 (each tab is its own sale)

        
F2Search or scan

        
F3Customer of the sale

        
F4Quantity of the selected line

        
F6Discount on the whole cart

        
F7 / F8Park cart / parked carts

        
F9Clear the invoice

        
F10Pay. In the payment window: exact cash; press again to finish

        
↑ ↓Select a line (or a search result)

        
+ -One more / one less of the selected line

        
DeleteRemove the selected line

        
3* + barcodeSell 3 pieces of the scanned product
